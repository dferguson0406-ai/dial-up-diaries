Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What this is

Dial-Up Diaries ("we") runs a YouTube channel about 90s nostalgia. This policy covers the "Dial-Up Diaries Uploader," a private tool we use to upload videos to our own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The uploader tool accesses only our own YouTube channel, using OAuth scopes (youtube.upload and youtube) granted by the channel owner. It uploads videos, sets titles/descriptions/thumbnails, and reads basic channel info needed to complete uploads.

Data we collect from others

None. This tool is used solely by the channel owner. We do not collect, store, or share data from any other person.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties.
